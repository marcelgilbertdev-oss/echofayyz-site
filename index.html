About

ECHOFAYYZ is worship you can feel in your chest. Christian songs written for the valley and the light after it — each in two versions, a euphoric EDM version and a slow, cinematic ballad — and dance anthems for the nights in between. Written, directed and produced by Marcel Gilbert, released through ZEROFAYYZ STUDIOS.

How these are made

AI tools are used to make this music and these videos, and that is disclosed everywhere it is published — on YouTube, on every Reel and Short, and here.

ECHOFAYYZ is not an AI persona. The lyrics are written and edited by hand, the videos are directed shot by shot, and every creative call is a person’s. The tools are tools. The artist is a person.
